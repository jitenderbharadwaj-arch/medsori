/ ABOUT US

A factory built around tolerance.

Medsor Impex started as a medical equipment supplier in 2016. Today our laser division serves over 180 active B2B clients across India — from EV startups to defense PSUs.

/01

Origin

We founded Medsor Impex in Delhi to supply hospital furniture and medical equipment to government tenders. Quickly, we realized that the bottleneck in delivering quality medical fabrication wasn't sourcing — it was finding a laser cutter and welder who treated tolerance as gospel.

So we built one in-house. Our laser division was born from that frustration and now runs as a standalone job-shop serving manufacturers who care about how clean the edge looks.

/02

Philosophy

Three rules govern every job that leaves our floor:

  • Measure twice, cut once. Every drawing goes through a CAD review before nesting. We catch errors at the screen, not the saw.
  • Tolerance is non-negotiable. ±0.05 mm isn't aspirational — it's the standard. Out-of-spec parts get re-run on our cost.
  • Delivery is part of quality. A perfect part that arrives late is a defective part.
